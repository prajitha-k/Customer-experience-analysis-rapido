# Customer-experience-analysis-rapido
Customer experience analysis of Rapido ride services using survey data collected through Google Forms. Analyzed customer preferences, satisfaction, ride behavior, and improvement areas using Excel and an interactive dashboard.
## Project Objectives
- Analyze customer experience and ride behaviour.
- Understand customers' preferred vehicle types and ride purposes.
- Analyze customers' preferred riding days.
- Identify commonly used payment methods.
- Analyze booking status distribution.
- Evaluate average ride ratings and customer loyalty.
- Identify major areas that require improvement.
- Present survey results through an interactive dashboard.
- Generate actionable insights that can help improve customer satisfaction.
## Data Collection

The dataset was created by conducting a Google Form survey to collect responses from Rapido users.

The survey collected information related to:

- Ride preferences
- Vehicle type
- Ride purpose
- Preferred days
- Payment method
- Booking status
- Customer ride rating
- Customer loyalty
- Waiting time
- Pricing
- Driver behaviour
- App-related issues

The collected Google Form responses were exported and prepared for analysis in Microsoft Excel.

## Project Process
1. Data Collection

A Google Form was created to collect responses from customers regarding their Rapido ride experience, preferences, satisfaction, and behaviour.

2. Data Preparation

The collected responses were exported from Google Forms and imported into Excel. The dataset was checked for missing values, inconsistencies, duplicate responses, and formatting issues.

3. Data Cleaning

The data was cleaned and organized to make it suitable for analysis. Relevant categorical and numerical fields were standardized.

4. Data Analysis

Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, and calculations were used to analyze customer responses and identify patterns and trends.

5. Dashboard Creation

An interactive dashboard was created with KPI cards, charts, and slicers to provide a clear overview of customer experience.

6. Insight Generation

The dashboard was analyzed to identify customer preferences, satisfaction levels, revenue patterns, and key areas requiring improvement.
## Project Insights

1.Vehicle Type

Among the vehicle types, Bike generated the highest revenue at approximately ₹1,40,289, followed by Cab at ₹1,14,662 and Auto at ₹77,030.

2.Ride Purpose

Personal rides were the most common ride purpose with 543 responses, followed by Shopping (215), College (191), and Emergency (150).

3.Preferred Days

Customers showed a stronger preference for weekend rides, with 437 responses. 393 customers preferred both weekdays and weekends equally, while 269 preferred weekdays.

4.Payment Method

Cash recorded the highest transaction value in the dashboard, followed by UPI, Card, and Wallet.

The findings highlight Bike rides, personal trips, and weekend preferences, while pricing, waiting time, and driver behaviour are key areas for improvement.
